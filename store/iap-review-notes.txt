NL-Noodpakket Pro is a one-time, non-consumable in-app purchase (product ID: pro_unlock). It is not a subscription.

What it unlocks:
(1) All emergency hacks: practical, step-by-step solutions using household items (15 of the 25 hacks; the other 10 stay free).
(2) Reminders: local notifications on the device for items that are due for replacement. Nothing is sent to a server.
(3) Unlimited documents in the on-device vault (free up to 3 documents).

How to reach the purchase screen (this is what the review screenshot shows):
1. Open the "Wat nu?" tab (bottom bar, third from the left).
2. Scroll down to the section "Extra hulp" and tap "Noodhacks".
3. Tap any hack that has the "Pro" badge, for example "Water vinden als de kraan droog is".
The purchase sheet opens on top of the hacks list. It shows the price from the App Store, "Eenmalig, geen abonnement" (one-time, no subscription), "Aankoop herstellen" (Restore Purchases), and "Niet nu" (Not now). The close button at the top right closes the sheet without any consequence.

Other places where the sheet can be opened:
- Reminders: open any item that has an expiry date and switch on "Herinner mij vóór het verloopt".
- Vault: in the vault, tap "Document toevoegen" to add a fourth document (the first three are free).
- Settings: "Meer" tab > "Instellingen" > "NL-Noodpakket Pro".

Restore Purchases is available in the purchase sheet and in "Meer" > "Instellingen" > "NL-Noodpakket Pro" ("Aankoop herstellen").

Free forever, without any purchase: all safety information and emergency step-by-step plans, the "first hour" card, the checklist and expiry overview, the emergency plan (including sharing and back-up), the vault up to 3 documents, all myth-busting hacks, and two sample hacks. The paywall never appears on these screens.

The app works fully offline. It needs no account or login. It has no ads and no tracking, and it does not collect personal data; all data stays on the device. The app is in Dutch only (it is made for households in the Netherlands).
